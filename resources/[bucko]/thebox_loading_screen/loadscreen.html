KEEP IT FAIR

Server Rules

01

Respect

Treat other players and staff with respect. No harassment, discrimination or targeted abuse.

02

Stay In Character

Keep roleplay immersive. Avoid breaking character without a genuine reason.

03

No Metagaming

Don't use information your character could not reasonably know.

04

No Powergaming

Give other players a fair chance to react. Don't force outcomes on someone else's character.

05

Value Your Life

Danger should matter. React realistically when your character is threatened.

06

Follow Staff Direction

If staff are handling an active issue, follow their instructions and use the correct report process.
